# Cost Model Framework

## Purpose

Define how project cost will be measured once prototype quantities and local procurement data are available. This document intentionally avoids inventing a final project price before the structure, foundation, water source, grid connection, and local fabrication cost are known.

## CAPEX structure

### A. Solar-tree structure

- steel column/mast;
- canopy frame;
- rails;
- bolts and connections;
- galvanizing/coating;
- fabrication labour;
- transport;
- erection;
- cranes/lifting;
- quality control.

### B. Foundations and civil works

- geotechnical investigation;
- excavation/drilling;
- concrete/rebar or piles;
- anchor systems;
- site grading;
- drainage;
- roads and service tracks.

### C. Photovoltaic system

- PV modules;
- DC cables;
- combiner equipment;
- disconnects and protection;
- inverters;
- monitoring;
- grounding/lightning protection.

### D. Electrical infrastructure

- MV transformers;
- switchgear;
- MV cables;
- substation;
- protection/SCADA;
- grid interconnection;
- transmission/export line where required.

### E. Water system

- hydrogeological studies;
- wells;
- pumps and VFDs;
- water treatment;
- reservoirs;
- mains;
- sector manifolds;
- drip irrigation;
- fertigation;
- water meters and sensors.

### F. Agricultural establishment

- soil testing;
- land preparation;
- soil amendments;
- seed/planting material;
- initial agronomy;
- equipment and field infrastructure.

### G. Digital and control layer

- weather stations;
- soil sensors;
- flow/pressure monitoring;
- communications;
- SCADA/data platform;
- asset-management system.

### H. Project costs

- engineering;
- permitting;
- environmental studies;
- project management;
- owner’s costs;
- contingency;
- insurance/security where required.

## OPEX structure

- PV cleaning;
- structural inspection;
- electrical maintenance;
- pump maintenance;
- well rehabilitation;
- irrigation maintenance;
- labour;
- crop inputs;
- monitoring/communications;
- security;
- spare parts;
- inverter replacement reserve;
- water-treatment consumables;
- insurance and administrative costs.

## Required unit metrics

The pilot should establish:

- USD/tree structure installed;
- kg steel/tree;
- m³ concrete/tree or foundation cost/tree;
- USD/kWp installed;
- USD/ha irrigation network;
- USD/m³/day pumping capacity;
- USD/m³ reservoir capacity;
- annual OPEX/tree;
- annual OPEX/ha;
- maintenance hours/tree/year.

## Economic outputs

The final feasibility model should separate revenues/value from:

1. electricity consumed internally;
2. electricity exported;
3. crop sales;
4. cold-storage/processing value where included;
5. water savings versus an open-sun control if measured.

## Decision metrics

- total CAPEX;
- annual OPEX;
- LCOE for electricity component;
- agricultural gross margin/ha;
- agricultural value/m³ water;
- combined project IRR/NPV where financing assumptions are defined;
- payback period;
- sensitivity to steel cost, water depth, crop value, PV yield, and grid price.

## Status

Framework only. No final CAPEX figure should be published until Phase 1 produces a measured bill of materials and credible local prices.
