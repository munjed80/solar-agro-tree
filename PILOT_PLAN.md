# Pilot Plan

## Objective

Reduce technical, agricultural, water-resource, and economic uncertainty before committing to a 1 km² deployment.

## Phase 1 — Full-scale single-tree prototype

Build one complete D11 tree at full scale.

Validate:

- fabrication method;
- steel weight;
- foundation concept;
- rail installation;
- module replacement;
- cable routing;
- drainage;
- wind response;
- access and maintenance;
- measured PV output by face;
- actual installed cost.

Required instrumentation should include structural and weather monitoring appropriate to the prototype.

### Exit criteria

Proceed only if the structure can be manufactured, installed, maintained, and certified at an economically credible cost.

## Phase 2 — Seven-hexagon / 31-tree pilot

Construct the central hexagon plus six adjacent hexagons sharing edges, producing 31 unique solar trees.

Primary goals:

- validate periodic shadow predictions;
- measure microclimate;
- test irrigation layout;
- compare crop groups;
- test electrical grouping and MPPT architecture;
- validate cleaning and maintenance logistics;
- measure soil response.

### Minimum agricultural experimental design

Include open-sun control plots and multiple shaded plots with replicated crops. Record water delivered to every treatment.

### Exit criteria

Proceed if measured crop/water performance supports a clear agricultural benefit and the technical system remains operable.

## Phase 3 — Approximately 10 ha demonstration

Indicative tree count at D11/S12 density:

- ~802 trees before road/service-layout adjustment.

Goals:

- commercial-scale field operations;
- tractors and logistics;
- sectorized irrigation;
- reservoir operation;
- production forecasting;
- maintenance staffing;
- electrical block architecture;
- real crop economics;
- water-accounting discipline.

### Recommended duration

At least two meaningful crop cycles, with seasonal variation sufficient to test heat and water-stress conditions.

## Phase 4 — 1 km² project

Proceed only after:

- structural design is independently reviewed;
- hydrogeological abstraction is demonstrably sustainable;
- crop trials meet water-productivity targets;
- PV yield is professionally modelled;
- grid/offtake strategy is confirmed;
- CAPEX/OPEX is updated from measured pilot costs;
- permits and land rights are secured.

## Deliverables at every phase

- design revision;
- measured data package;
- cost report;
- risk register;
- lessons learned;
- go/no-go decision memo.

## Governance

Pilot data should be preserved as an auditable engineering dataset. Assumptions must be updated when measured data becomes available rather than keeping outdated concept values.
